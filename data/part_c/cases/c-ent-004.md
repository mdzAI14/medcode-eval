Ear, nose and throat clinic, new patient visit

History: 63-year-old man with trouble hearing in both ears that has come on slowly over several years. He worked for thirty years in a metal stamping plant with loud machinery and wore hearing protection only some of the time. He retired two years ago. No ear pain, no discharge, no ringing in the ears and no dizziness. No ear surgery. No other medical problems and no regular medicines.

Examination: Ear canals and eardrums normal in both ears. Tuning fork tests: Weber test in the midline, air conduction better than bone conduction in both ears.

Audiogram today, reviewed by me: sensorineural hearing loss in both ears, symmetric, with a notch at 4000 hertz reaching 55 decibels. Word recognition 88 percent in each ear.

Assessment: Sensorineural hearing loss, bilateral.

Plan: Referred for a hearing aid evaluation. Repeat audiogram in one year.
