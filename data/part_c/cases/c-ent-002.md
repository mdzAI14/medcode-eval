Ear, nose and throat clinic, new patient visit

History: 52-year-old woman with one week of brief spinning episodes, each lasting under a minute. They come on when she turns to the right in bed and when she looks up. No hearing loss, no ringing in the ears and no ear fullness. No headache, weakness or trouble speaking. No other medical problems and no regular medicines.

Examination: Ear canals and eardrums normal. Tuning fork tests normal. Cranial nerves normal. Gait normal. Dix-Hallpike test to the right brings on vertigo with torsional nystagmus for about 20 seconds. To the left it is negative.

Assessment: Vertigo, probable benign paroxysmal positional vertigo of the right ear.

Treatment: Canalith repositioning (Epley) maneuver for the right ear. The Dix-Hallpike test repeated afterwards was negative and she felt better.

Plan: Return in two weeks if the episodes continue.
