- 29-year-old woman, new patient visit in the ear, nose and throat clinic.
- Ten days of nasal congestion, thick yellow-green nasal discharge and facial pressure. She improved after a cold and then got worse again over the last four days.
- No fever. First episode of this kind, no previous sinus infections.
- No allergies, no asthma, has never smoked.
- Examination: tenderness to percussion over both cheeks, purulent discharge in both nasal passages, no polyps, septum in the midline, throat and ears normal.
- No imaging was done.
- Assessment in the physician's words: acute sinusitis. The physician does not name a sinus.
- Plan: amoxicillin-clavulanate for seven days, saline rinses, return if not better.
- No other medical problems and no regular medicines.
