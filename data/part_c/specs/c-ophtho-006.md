- Assessment in the ophthalmologist's words: glaucoma suspect, both eyes: open angle with borderline findings, low risk; stable; no treatment needed.
- Plan: no drops; return in one year with visual fields and imaging.
- The ophthalmologist does not diagnose glaucoma or ocular hypertension. The assessment itself states the open angle, the borderline findings and the low risk, in the ophthalmologist's words.
