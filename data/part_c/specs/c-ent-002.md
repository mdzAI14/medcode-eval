- 52-year-old woman, new patient visit in the ear, nose and throat clinic.
- One week of brief spinning episodes, each lasting under a minute, brought on by turning to the right in bed and by looking up.
- No hearing loss, no ringing in the ears, no ear fullness, no headache, no weakness and no trouble speaking.
- Examination: ear canals and eardrums normal, tuning fork tests normal, cranial nerves normal, gait normal.
- Dix-Hallpike test to the right brings on vertigo with torsional nystagmus for about 20 seconds; to the left it is negative.
- Assessment in the physician's words: vertigo, probable benign paroxysmal positional vertigo of the right ear.
- Treatment at the visit: canalith repositioning (Epley) maneuver for the right ear. The Dix-Hallpike test repeated afterwards was negative and she felt better.
- Plan: return in two weeks if the episodes continue.
- No other medical problems and no regular medicines.
