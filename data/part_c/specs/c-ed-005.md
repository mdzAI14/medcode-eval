- Two days of burning when she passes urine.
- No fever, no back or flank pain, no blood in the urine, no vaginal discharge. She is not pregnant.
- Examination: temperature 36.9 C, heart rate 78, blood pressure 116/72; abdomen soft with no tenderness; no tenderness over the kidneys.
- Urine dipstick done and recorded by the nurse: leukocyte esterase positive, nitrite positive, no blood.
- A urine culture was sent. No result is available at this visit.
- Diagnosis in the emergency physician's words: dysuria; suspected urinary tract infection.
- Disposition: nitrofurantoin for five days; she will be called with the culture result; return for fever or flank pain.

- No other medical problems and no regular medicines.
