- 61-year-old man, follow-up visit in the ear, nose and throat clinic.
- Meniere's disease of the right ear, diagnosed four years ago.
- Treatment, both continuing: a low-salt diet, and hydrochlorothiazide with triamterene every day.
- He has had no vertigo attacks in the last twelve months. No change in his hearing and no new ringing in the ear.
- He has no hypertension; the diuretic is taken for the Meniere's disease.
- Examination: blood pressure 122/76; ear canals and eardrums normal in both ears; no nystagmus; balance testing in the office is normal.
- Assessment in the physician's words: Meniere's disease of the right ear, well controlled on current treatment.
- Plan: continue the low-salt diet and the diuretic; return in one year, sooner if the attacks come back.
- The physician does not call the disease resolved or cured, and the treatment is not stopped.
- No other medical problems and no other regular medicines.
