- 29-year-old male presents to the emergency department after falling from a ladder while painting a house.
- He fell approximately 8 feet onto a concrete driveway and landed on his right arm.
- He has immediate pain and swelling of the right wrist; no loss of consciousness, no head injury, no other injuries.
- Examination: right wrist deformity, tenderness over the distal radius, no open wound, radial pulse intact, sensation intact in the median, ulnar, and radial nerve distributions.
- X-ray of the right wrist shows a closed, displaced fracture of the distal radius.
- No ulnar fracture, no carpal fracture, no dislocation.
- He was evaluated and treated in the emergency department with closed reduction and splint placement.
- This is the first evaluation and treatment for this injury.
- Tetanus immunization is up to date.
- Orthopedic follow-up arranged.
