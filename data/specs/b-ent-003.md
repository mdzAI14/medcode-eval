- 6-year-old girl seen in the outpatient otolaryngology clinic; office visit.
- Reason for the encounter: 1 year of mouth breathing and snoring; evaluation of tonsil and adenoid size.
- This is her first visit to otolaryngology for this problem.
- Examination: tonsils 3+ and touching (kissing tonsils); no exudate, no erythema. The provider ties the tonsillar size to the tonsillar hypertrophy.
- Nasal endoscopy: adenoid tissue narrowing the choanae, obstructing about 80% of the nasopharyngeal airway. The provider ties this to the adenoid hypertrophy.
- No fever, no cervical lymphadenopathy.
- No history of recurrent tonsillitis; no sore throat in the past year.
- There is no tonsillitis and no adenoiditis.
- No diagnosis of obstructive sleep apnea is made.
- No imaging or labs.
- No other conditions are documented.
- The condition is chronic. No cause is stated.
- The provider documents one diagnosis: "Hypertrophy of tonsils with hypertrophy of adenoids."
