- 58-year-old male with a 12-year history of type 2 diabetes mellitus, managed with metformin and insulin, presents with blurred vision in the right eye for 2 months.
- Best-corrected visual acuity: right eye 20/60, left eye 20/25.
- Dilated fundus examination of the right eye shows proliferative diabetic retinopathy with neovascularization of the disc and macular edema involving the center of the macula.
- Left eye shows mild nonproliferative diabetic retinopathy without macular edema.
- No vitreous hemorrhage or tractional retinal detachment in either eye.
- No history of eye trauma, surgery, or intravitreal injection.
- HbA1c 8.9% today.
- Plan: intravitreal anti-VEGF injection in the right eye and continued diabetes management.
