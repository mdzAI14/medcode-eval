- 74-year-old female seen in the ophthalmology clinic for gradual blurring of vision in the right eye over 1 year.
- She reports glare and difficulty driving at night.
- This is her first visit to this clinic for the cataract.
- No prior eye surgery on the right eye.
- The left eye had cataract surgery in 2019.
- Visual acuity: right eye 20/50, left eye 20/25; the provider ties the reduced right-eye acuity to the right cataract.
- Slit-lamp examination, right eye: nuclear sclerosis grade 3.
- Slit-lamp examination, left eye: pseudophakic, posterior chamber intraocular lens in place, posterior capsule clear.
- Dilated fundus examination: normal in both eyes; no macular edema; the provider states these are normal findings.
- No imaging or labs.
- The provider describes the right cataract as visually significant.
- No cause for the cataract is documented.
- No cataract is diagnosed in the left eye, and the right eye is not pseudophakic.
- No other conditions are documented.
- Diagnoses as documented: "Nuclear sclerotic cataract, right eye." and "Pseudophakia, left eye."
