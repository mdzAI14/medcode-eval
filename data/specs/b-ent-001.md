- 34-year-old female admitted with 4 days of severe sore throat, fever, muffled voice, and difficulty swallowing.
- Examination: trismus, uvular deviation to the left, and a large right peritonsillar abscess with fluctuance.
- Vital signs: temperature 39.1°C, HR 124, BP 88/56, respiratory rate 24, oxygen saturation 96% on room air.
- Laboratory: lactate 3.2 mmol/L, WBC 19.8 x 10^9/L.
- Blood cultures grew Streptococcus pyogenes.
- CT neck with contrast confirms right peritonsillar abscess without extension into deep neck spaces.
- The patient was diagnosed with sepsis due to Streptococcus pyogenes originating from the peritonsillar abscess.
- She underwent incision and drainage of the right peritonsillar abscess and received intravenous antibiotics and fluids.
- No history of trauma, surgery, or immunosuppression.
