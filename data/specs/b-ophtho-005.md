- 47-year-old male seen in the outpatient ophthalmology clinic; office visit.
- 5 days of redness and irritation at the inner corner of the right eye.
- Examination, right eye: raised yellowish lesion of the nasal conjunctiva (a pinguecula) with surrounding redness and swelling; cornea clear; no discharge.
- Examination, left eye: normal.
- Visual acuity 20/20 in both eyes.
- The provider states the pinguecula is inflamed.
- The provider lists two diagnoses: "Pingueculitis, right eye." and "Pinguecula, right eye."
- No other conditions are documented.
- Plan: lubricating drops, a short course of steroid eye drops, follow-up in 2 weeks.
