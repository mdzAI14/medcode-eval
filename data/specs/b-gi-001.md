- 55-year-old male admitted with 2 days of melena and lightheadedness.
- Vital signs: BP 102/64, HR 108, afebrile.
- Hemoglobin 7.8 g/dL on admission.
- Upper endoscopy shows a 1.5 cm chronic gastric ulcer with active oozing from the ulcer base.
- The surrounding gastric mucosa shows acute gastritis with bleeding.
- No esophageal varices, no duodenal ulcer, no Mallory-Weiss tear.
- Helicobacter pylori stool antigen positive.
- No NSAID use, no alcohol use, no liver disease.
- He received intravenous fluids, proton pump inhibitor infusion, and endoscopic hemostasis.
- Discharge diagnoses: chronic gastric ulcer with hemorrhage; acute gastritis with bleeding.
