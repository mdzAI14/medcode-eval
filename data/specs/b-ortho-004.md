- 46-year-old male seen in the outpatient orthopedic clinic; office visit.
- 5 weeks of low back pain radiating down the right leg to the foot, with numbness of the right great toe.
- No injury. No bowel or bladder symptoms.
- Examination: positive straight leg raise on the right; decreased sensation over the right L5 dermatome; strength normal; left leg normal.
- MRI of the lumbar spine: right paracentral disc herniation at L4-L5 contacting the right L5 nerve root; no spinal stenosis; other levels normal.
- The provider states the radiculopathy is due to the disc herniation.
- The provider lists two diagnoses: "Lumbar disc herniation, L4-L5." and "Right lumbar radiculopathy."
- No other conditions are documented.
- Plan: physical therapy, a 10-day course of naproxen, follow-up in 6 weeks.
