- 58-year-old male presents to the emergency department with 3 days of redness, warmth and swelling of the right lower leg, with fever.
- This is his first visit for this episode.
- The provider states there is no injury, no open wound, no ulcer and no skin breakdown.
- Vital signs: temperature 38.3 C, heart rate 96, blood pressure 132/80.
- Examination of the right lower leg: erythema, warmth and tenderness; no abscess, no fluctuance, no drainage, no ulcer, no open wound. The provider ties these findings to the cellulitis.
- The area of erythema was outlined.
- Left lower leg: normal, apart from a healed scar on the left shin from an injury in 2019.
- WBC 13.2 x 10^9/L; the provider ties it to the cellulitis.
- No imaging, no cultures.
- Essential hypertension, on hydrochlorothiazide; addressed this visit (medication continued).
- No diabetes.
- No cause for the cellulitis is stated.
- Diagnosis as documented: "Cellulitis of the right lower extremity."
