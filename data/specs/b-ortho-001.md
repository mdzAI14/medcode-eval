- 67-year-old female presents to orthopedic clinic with 8 months of right knee pain, worse with walking and stairs.
- No history of knee trauma, infection, or surgery.
- Examination: right knee medial joint line tenderness, crepitus, mild effusion, range of motion 5–115 degrees.
- Left knee is asymptomatic.
- Weight-bearing radiographs of the right knee show moderate medial compartment joint space narrowing, subchondral sclerosis, and marginal osteophytes.
- No fracture, dislocation, or acute bony abnormality.
- Diagnosis: primary osteoarthritis of the right knee.
- Plan: physical therapy, intra-articular corticosteroid injection, and follow-up.
